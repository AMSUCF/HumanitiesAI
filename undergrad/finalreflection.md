## Final Portfolio and Reflection

Over seven modules, you've talked with chatbots old and new, fact-checked generated writing, read texts at scale, generated and critiqued images, described archival collections, built a mini-exhibit, and designed a study companion of your own. Along the way, you've been practicing the core moves of digital humanities: asking humanities questions about technology, checking what tools tell you, and learning by making.

This final assignment asks you to gather that work, improve one piece of it, and reflect on where you're headed.

### Part One: Your Portfolio

Choose **three** exercises from the semester that best represent your work and learning. For each one, include:

- A link to your original discussion post (or the screenshots, links, or images you shared)
- A two- or three-sentence caption explaining what the exercise was and why you chose it

### Part Two: Revise or Extend One Exercise

Choose **one** of your three exercises and take it further. Some options:

- Redo a module's exercise with a different tool, and compare
- Fact-check or test something you didn't get to the first time
- Add new texts, images, or features to your Module Three, Five, or Six work
- Revise your Module Seven study companion based on your classmates' feedback

Share the revised work along with a short paragraph explaining what you changed and why.

### Discussion

Post your portfolio (Part One), your revised work (Part Two), and a reflection of **750-1000 words**. Your reflection should respond to the following questions, citing at least two readings from the semester:

- **Where did you start?** What did you think about generative AI at the beginning of the semester, and what has changed (or been confirmed)?
- **What did you learn from making things?** Which exercise taught you the most about how these tools work, or where they fall short? What did revising your work show you?
- **What will you carry forward?** How do you plan to use (or not use) AI tools in your classes, your work, or your creative life? Which digital humanities habit from this course (checking sources, examining power, reading at scale, building to think, or another) do you expect to keep using?

You may include AI-generated material in your portfolio, but your reflection itself should be your own writing. Replies to classmates are not required for this final post, but they are encouraged as we close out the semester together.

A blurry JPEG

Ted Chiang on compression

quote.txt
Think of ChatGPT as a blurry JPEG of all the text on the Web. It retains much of the information on the Web, in the same way that a JPEG retains much of the information of a higher-resolution image, but, if you’re looking for an exact sequence of bits, you won’t find it; all you will ever get is an approximation.

Ted Chiang, 2023

quote.txt
What led to problems was the fact that the photocopier was producing numbers that were readable but incorrect; it made the copies seem accurate when they weren’t.

Ted Chiang, 2023

Chiang opens with a Xerox photocopier that silently swapped numbers on building plans. That's the model for a hallucination: plausible, readable, wrong.
quote.txt
These hallucinations are compression artifacts, but—like the incorrect labels generated by the Xerox photocopier—they are plausible enough that identifying them requires comparing them against the originals, which in this case means either the Web or our own knowledge of the world.

Ted Chiang, 2023

Four steps

  • Pick a narrow topic you already know
  • Ask for an overview + five scholarly sources, search off
  • Check every source in the UCF Library and Google Scholar
  • Ask again with search on, and check again
prompt.exe

steps 2 and 4

> Write a 300-word overview of [your topic] for a college student. Then list five scholarly sources (books or journal articles) I could read to learn more, with full citations.

Sort every source

  • Real and accurate: exists, correct details, actually on topic
  • Real but wrong: exists, but the details or the topic are off
  • Fabricated: no evidence it exists

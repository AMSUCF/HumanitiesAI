Next week, Do: Build a Mini-Exhibit

watch the video, then do the exercise and post by Thursday

Five steps

  • Plan: what it is, what goes in it, what a visitor can do
  • Prompt: describe it specifically and paste in your material
  • Revise at least five times, and test every change
  • Look under the hood: ask the AI to explain the code
  • Share the link, and open it in a private window
Claude artifacts, ChatGPT canvas, and Gemini Canvas all work on free tiers.
prompt.exe

an example first prompt

> Build a single-page interactive web exhibit called “Cats in the Library of Congress.” Show these six images as a gallery. Clicking an image opens a panel with its title, date, and a two-sentence description. Add buttons to sort by date or type. Include alt text for every image.

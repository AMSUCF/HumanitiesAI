Exercise: Take Me to the Land of Jazz

Two Workflows, One Song

Animation (Claude or ChatGPT): attach the MP3 and ask for “an artistic, silly symphony inspired, web animation using p5 brushstrokes … that echoes the lyrics and timing.”

Video (Gemini): “Review the lyrics for Take Me to the Land of Jazz (egrove.olemiss.edu/sharris_c/163/) and generate a silly symphony inspired video that reflects the song’s story.”

Gemini can’t take the audio file, so it follows the words, not the music. Ask for a 20-second version.

The Land of Jazz: p5.js animation by Claude Opus 5.5 (Extra effort), drawn in code and timed to the 1919 recording

Gemini (Veo 3.1), from the lyrics alone: 20 seconds of generated pixels

Whose Land of Jazz?

  • The prompt never says who lives there. The model fills the gap from its training data.
  • Jazz grew out of Black American musical traditions; cartoons of the era often drew Black performers as racist caricatures.
  • Noble: these systems are not neutral windows. Whose version of the song do we see, and who is missing or distorted?
  • “Silly symphony” names a studio tradition and the animators who built it.
